Canva Founder - Melanie Perkins

Melanie Perkins was born in 1987 in Perth, Western Australia, into a modest, middle-class multicultural family. Her father, an engineer of Malaysian–Filipino heritage, instilled in her a sense of technical curiosity and problem-solving skills, while her mother, a teacher of Australian descent, nurtured creativity, discipline, and a love for learning. She grew up with siblings in a supportive household that valued education, hard work, and practical life skills. Although the family was comfortably middle class, they were not wealthy; vacations were simple, and entrepreneurship was not a family tradition. This upbringing, combined with her multicultural background, fostered a mindset of resourcefulness, humility, and curiosity—qualities that would later help her navigate the global entrepreneurial journey of building Canva.

Nike Founder - Phil Knight

Phil Knight was born on February 24, 1938, in Portland, Oregon, into a middle-class American family. His father, William W. This family support and sports culture became the foundation for his entrepreneurial journey in the athletic shoe industry.

Paytm Founder - Vijay Shekhar Sharma

Vijay Shekhar Sharma, the founder of Paytm, was born in 1978 in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, into a humble lower middle-class family. The youngest of four children, Vijay grew up in a joint family environment that emphasized hard work, resilience, and living within limited means. The family’s economic status was modest, residing in a small home where comfort was secondary to education. These early years of financial constraints and minimal urban exposure shaped Vijay’s character, fostering grit, self-reliance, and a willingness to take risks that later defined his entrepreneurial journey.
