Questions people ask

Is Findme free?

Yes. Both Quick and Detailed Findme are free. You only need to log in and add a few details to see the full report, so we can save it to your account.

What's the difference between Quick and Detailed Findme?

Both use the same 105-question bank and adapt to your answers. Quick Findme asks 6 to 8 questions for a fast first read (if you are logged in and your profile has your studies and skills, it can use those instead). Detailed Findme asks 15 to 25 questions about what you enjoy, how you work and what matters to you, and gives the most accurate result.

Is Findme an IQ or aptitude test?

No. It looks at your interests, personality and values, so there are no right or wrong answers. Treat the result as a starting point to explore, not a final verdict.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The tools work inside your browser, so your photos and documents never leave your device.
