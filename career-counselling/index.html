CLASS 8 TO WORKING PROFESSIONALS · PICK YOUR OWN DEPTH

Career counselling that ends with a plan

Choosing a stream, a college or a new career is one of the biggest decisions you'll make. We help you understand your options, what each one costs and leads to, and what to do next, at a depth and price that suits you.

Start the free assessment See options and prices

Who it's for

Class 8 to 10

Choosing between Science, Commerce and Humanities, and knowing what each one leads to.

Class 11 and 12

Picking a course and college, planning entrance exams, and having a backup plan.

College students

Choosing a specialisation, internships, higher studies vs a job, and building the right skills.

Working professionals

Changing careers, moving up, or going back to study, without losing what you've built.

Parents

Understanding your child's options, costs and timelines, so you can support the decision together.

What we help you work out

  • Which stream or degree fits your interests, strengths and marks
  • Entrance exams to take, their dates and how to prepare
  • Colleges to shortlist, with fees, cut-offs and location
  • Total cost of each path, scholarships and education loans
  • Careers each path leads to, and the demand for them
  • A backup plan if the first choice doesn't work out
  • Skills, certifications and projects to start now
  • Studying abroad vs in India
  • Switching careers and what it takes

How it works

  1. Take the free assessment

    13 questions about your interests, strengths, working style and values. About 6 minutes.

  2. Choose your depth

    Keep the free result, get the detailed report, or book a one-to-one session.

  3. Talk it through

    In a session, a counsellor goes through your answers, marks, budget and family situation with you.

  4. Leave with a written plan

    Your options, exams, colleges and a month-by-month plan you can follow at home.

Questions

Do I need to take the free assessment first?

It helps. The counsellor can start from your result instead of from zero, so the session goes deeper. But you can book a session directly.

Can parents join the session?

Yes, and we recommend it, especially for Class 8 to 12. Decisions about streams and colleges work best when the family is part of them.

What's the difference between the report and a session?

The report is ready straight away and covers stream fit, exams and colleges for your result. A session adds a real conversation about your marks, budget and worries, and ends with a plan made for you.

How do I book, and what happens after I click?

Choose an option and we note your request. Our team gets back to you to fix a time and confirm the price. You can also message us on WhatsApp.

Is the session online?

Sessions are on a video or phone call, so you can join from anywhere.
